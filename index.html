Privacy Policy — Denny Horoskop

Last updated: September 8, 2026

1. Overview

Denny Horoskop is an astrology calculation application (natal charts, transits, annual horoscopes, directions, solar returns, synastry). The app operates fully offline: it does not require an account, it does not connect to any server of ours, and it does not transmit any data you enter.

2. Data we collect: none

We do not collect, store or process any personal data on our side. There are no accounts, no login, no analytics, and no telemetry. All information you enter (names, dates, times and places of birth) is stored exclusively on your device, in the app's local storage. When you uninstall the application, this data is removed with it.

3. Your data export

The app lets you export your saved data to a file. That file is created on your device and remains entirely under your control — it is never sent anywhere by the application.

4. Third-party service: advertising

The only network communication in the app is performed by the embedded Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK, which loads and displays advertisements. When an ad is loaded, the SDK may communicate with Google servers and process, in accordance with Google's own policies:

  • the advertising identifier of your device (GAID),
  • device and OS information needed to deliver the ad,
  • network information,
  • location data, only if you have granted location permission.

Details of Google's data processing are available in the Google Privacy Policy. You can limit personalized advertising in your device settings (Android: Settings → Ads → “Opt out of Ad Personalization”).

In short: the application itself sends no data of yours anywhere. Your horoscope data never leaves your phone — it stays on the device or in an export file you create.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page always reflects the latest version.
